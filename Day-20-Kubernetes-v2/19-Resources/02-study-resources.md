## Practice method

For every resource, answer:

1. What problem does it solve?
2. What happens if it fails?
3. How is it monitored?
4. How is it secured?
5. How does it scale?
6. How is it rolled back?
7. What is the alternative?

The goal is engineering understanding, not YAML memorization.
