# Data Product Discovery

## Purpose
Treat discoverable datasets as products with explicit expectations.

## Data Product Card

A useful data product card includes:
- product name
- business purpose
- owner
- steward
- schema
- SLA/SLO
- freshness
- quality score
- classification
- approved consumers
- lineage
- support channel
- change policy

## Automobile Product

**Certified Vehicle Telemetry Daily**

Purpose: fleet engineering and approved analytics.

Inputs: vehicle event streams.

Outputs: curated daily vehicle metrics.

Controls: schema validation, quality checks, classification, access policy, lineage,
retention, and owner approval.
