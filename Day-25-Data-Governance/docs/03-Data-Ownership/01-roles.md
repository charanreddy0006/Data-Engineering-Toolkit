# Data Governance Roles

## Purpose
Understand the people accountable for governed data.

## Data Owner

The data owner is accountable for business decisions about a data domain or asset,
including acceptable use, classification, quality expectations, and access decisions.

## Data Steward

The steward handles day-to-day governance activities such as definitions, metadata,
quality issue coordination, and policy interpretation.

## Data Custodian

The custodian operates technical infrastructure and implements controls. The
custodian does not automatically become the business owner.

## Producer and Consumer

A producer creates or publishes data. A consumer uses data for an approved purpose.
A team can play more than one role.
