# Scenario-Based Governance Interviews

## Purpose
Practice realistic problem-solving.

## Scenario 1

A dashboard shows different vehicle counts in three departments. Explain how you
would investigate definitions, ownership, lineage, source authority, transformations,
and quality before changing the dashboard.

## Scenario 2

A producer wants to remove a telemetry field used by five downstream teams. Use
lineage, impact analysis, contract compatibility, communication, and migration planning.

## Scenario 3

An analyst requests raw customer-linked location data. Evaluate purpose, minimum
necessary fields, classification, owner approval, access controls, and retention.
