# Privacy by Design

## Purpose
Build privacy considerations into data products instead of adding them at the end.

## Practical Techniques

- collect only what is necessary
- define purpose
- minimize identifiers
- restrict access
- use aggregation where possible
- document retention
- protect data throughout its lifecycle
- provide appropriate deletion/rights workflows where applicable

## Automotive Example

A fleet analytics product may not need raw precise customer location. A coarser or
aggregated representation may satisfy the business need while reducing exposure.
