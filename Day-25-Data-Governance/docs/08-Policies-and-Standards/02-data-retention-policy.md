# Data Retention Governance

## Purpose
Design retention rules that are explicit and reviewable.

## Retention Model

For each data class or dataset define:
- purpose
- retention period or trigger
- legal/business basis
- archival rule
- deletion mechanism
- exception process
- owner
- evidence of execution

## Automobile Example

Raw high-volume telemetry may have different retention requirements from aggregated
fleet KPIs. Do not automatically retain everything forever simply because storage is
cheap.
