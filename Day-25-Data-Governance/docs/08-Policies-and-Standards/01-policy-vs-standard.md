# Policy vs Standard vs Procedure

## Purpose
Understand three common governance document types.

## Policy

A policy states organizational intent and mandatory high-level rules.

## Standard

A standard defines specific mandatory requirements used to implement a policy.

## Procedure

A procedure gives step-by-step instructions for performing an operational task.

## Example

```text
Policy: Sensitive data must be protected.
   |
Standard: Restricted datasets require approved identity controls and audit logging.
   |
Procedure: Steps for requesting, approving, provisioning, and reviewing access.
```
