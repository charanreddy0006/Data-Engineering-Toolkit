# Governance Scorecard

## Purpose
Build a domain-level governance scorecard.

## Example

| Dimension | Weight | Score |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ownership | 20% | 90 |
| Metadata | 15% | 80 |
| Lineage | 15% | 75 |
| Classification | 15% | 95 |
| Access reviews | 15% | 88 |
| Quality governance | 10% | 82 |
| Contracts/change | 10% | 70 |

## Interpretation

A score is a conversation starter, not a substitute for risk analysis. A single
critical policy violation may matter more than a high aggregate score.
