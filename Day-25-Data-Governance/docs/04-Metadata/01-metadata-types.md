# Types of Metadata

## Purpose
Understand the information that makes data understandable and governable.

## Business Metadata

Business definitions, owners, glossary terms, approved use, criticality, and domain.

## Technical Metadata

Tables, columns, types, schemas, locations, partitions, jobs, versions, and
transformation information.

## Operational Metadata

Pipeline runs, freshness, row counts, failures, latency, and operational status.

## Governance Metadata

Classification, policy links, retention, certification, stewardship, approvals,
exceptions, and regulatory attributes.

## Why It Matters

Metadata connects human understanding with machine execution:

`Business meaning -> Policy -> Technical asset -> Control -> Evidence`
