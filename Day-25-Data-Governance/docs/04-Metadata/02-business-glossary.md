# Business Glossary

## Purpose
Create a shared vocabulary for business concepts.

## Term Template

A useful glossary term contains:
- term name
- definition
- business domain
- owner
- steward
- synonyms
- examples
- calculation logic where applicable
- related terms
- approval status

## Automobile Terms

`Active Vehicle` should have an agreed definition rather than each team creating its
own interpretation.

Similarly, `Warranty Claim`, `Service Visit`, `Connected Vehicle`, and `Fast Charge`
need explicit meanings when they are used for enterprise reporting.
