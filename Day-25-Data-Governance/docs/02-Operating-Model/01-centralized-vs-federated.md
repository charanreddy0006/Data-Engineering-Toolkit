# Centralized, Federated, and Hybrid Governance

## Purpose
Compare governance operating models.

## Centralized

A central team owns most governance decisions.

Advantages:
- consistent standards
- easier enterprise reporting
- clear central authority

Risks:
- slow domain decisions
- weak domain context
- bottleneck at the center

## Federated

Domain teams own governance within shared enterprise principles.

Advantages:
- strong business context
- faster domain decisions
- ownership close to data

Risks:
- inconsistent standards
- duplicated processes
- difficult cross-domain alignment

## Hybrid

A central governance function defines enterprise-wide guardrails while domains own
day-to-day data decisions.

For large data platforms, hybrid governance is often a practical compromise.
