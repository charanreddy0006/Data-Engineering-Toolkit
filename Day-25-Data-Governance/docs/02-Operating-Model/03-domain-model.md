# Data Domain Model

## Purpose
Organize ownership around business domains.

## Example Domains

```text
Automotive Enterprise
├── Vehicle
├── Customer
├── Dealer
├── Manufacturing
├── Service
├── Parts
├── Warranty
├── Connected Vehicle
└── Energy / EV
```

## Why Domains Matter

A domain gives a business context for definitions, ownership, quality rules,
classification, and data products.

## Domain Boundary

A domain should have an accountable owner, defined datasets/data products, critical
terms, policies, quality expectations, and escalation paths.
