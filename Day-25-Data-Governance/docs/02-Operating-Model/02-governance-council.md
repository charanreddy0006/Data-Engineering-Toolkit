# Data Governance Council

## Purpose
Design a governance decision-making forum.

## Responsibilities

- approve enterprise policies
- resolve cross-domain conflicts
- prioritize governance initiatives
- review risk and compliance metrics
- establish decision rights
- approve exceptions above delegated authority

## Membership

Typical members can include data leadership, security/privacy, architecture,
business-domain representatives, legal/compliance, and platform engineering.
Exact membership is organization-specific.

## Meeting Agenda

1. KPI review
2. Critical incidents
3. Policy changes
4. New high-risk data products
5. Exceptions
6. Cross-domain decisions
7. Actions and owners
