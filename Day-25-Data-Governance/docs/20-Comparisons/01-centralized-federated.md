# Centralized vs Federated Governance

## Purpose
Choose an operating model deliberately.

## Comparison

| Dimension | Centralized | Federated |
|---|---|---|
| Consistency | High | Medium |
| Domain context | Medium | High |
| Local speed | Lower | Higher |
| Enterprise coordination | Easier | Harder |
| Bottleneck risk | Higher | Lower |
| Best fit | Smaller/standardized orgs | Large domain-oriented orgs |

## Practical Recommendation

Consider a hybrid model when enterprise guardrails must be consistent but business
domains need local decision-making.
