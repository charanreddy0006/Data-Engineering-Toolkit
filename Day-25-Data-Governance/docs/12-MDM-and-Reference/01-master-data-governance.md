# Master Data Governance

## Purpose
Understand governance of shared business entities.

## Master Data

Master data represents important business entities such as:
- vehicle
- customer
- dealer
- product
- supplier

## Governance Questions

Which system is authoritative? What identifiers are stable? Who resolves duplicates?
Who approves changes? How are downstream systems synchronized?

## Automobile Example

Vehicle master may contain VIN, vehicle model, production date, market, and lifecycle
status. Different systems must not silently invent incompatible meanings for the same
vehicle.
