# Automotive Governance Charter

## Purpose

Establish accountability and control for automotive data products.

## Scope

Vehicle, connected-vehicle, telemetry, customer, location, service, warranty,
dealer, and EV data.

## Objectives

1. Improve data trust.
2. Make critical data discoverable.
3. Reduce unmanaged access.
4. Make ownership explicit.
5. Enable safe schema evolution.
6. Produce auditable governance evidence.

## Decision Rights

- Domain owners approve business definitions and acceptable use.
- Stewards maintain governance metadata and coordinate issues.
- Custodians implement technical controls.
- Security/privacy functions advise on risk and controls.
- Governance council resolves cross-domain conflicts.

## Review

Critical governance metrics are reviewed regularly. Policies are reviewed at the
defined organizational cadence and after significant changes.
