## Completion Standard

Do not consider the topic complete until you can explain:

```text
Who owns the data?
What does it mean?
Where did it come from?
Who can use it?
How long can it be retained?
How do we know it is trustworthy?
What happens when it changes?
How do we prove governance is working?
```
