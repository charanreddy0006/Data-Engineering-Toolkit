# CI/CD Interview — Beginner

## What is CI?
Frequent integration with automated validation.

## What is CD?
Continuous Delivery or Continuous Deployment depending on context.

## What is a runner?
The environment executing pipeline jobs.

## What is an artifact?
A release/build output such as a package or container image.

## Artifact vs cache?
Artifact is release truth; cache is optimization.

## Why test in CI?
To catch issues before release.

## Why Docker?
Consistent application packaging.

## What is rollback?
Returning to a known previous artifact/deployment.

## Why protect secrets?
Repositories and logs can expose sensitive values.
