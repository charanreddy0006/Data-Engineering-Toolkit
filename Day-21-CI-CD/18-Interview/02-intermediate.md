# CI/CD Interview — Intermediate

## Why build once and promote?
The artifact tested in staging is exactly what reaches production.

## What should data-engineering CI test?
Python, SQL, schemas, contracts, transformations and data quality.

## Why are ETL retries dangerous?
They can repeat writes and create duplicates.

## What is a data-quality gate?
A release condition based on data correctness metrics.

## How do you handle database migrations?
Use backward-compatible expand/contract patterns where practical.

## What is OIDC?
An identity mechanism that can provide short-lived cloud credentials without storing long-lived keys.
