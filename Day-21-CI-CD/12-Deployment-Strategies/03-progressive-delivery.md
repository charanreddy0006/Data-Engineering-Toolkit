# Progressive Delivery

Progressive delivery gradually exposes a release.

```text
new release
 ↓
small exposure
 ↓
observe
 ↓
increase
 ↓
100%
```

Signals can include:

- error rate,
- latency,
- throughput,
- resource usage,
- business metrics.

For data pipelines:

- record failures,
- freshness,
- duplicates,
- quality score,
- consumer lag.

Automation is only as good as the signals used to make decisions.
