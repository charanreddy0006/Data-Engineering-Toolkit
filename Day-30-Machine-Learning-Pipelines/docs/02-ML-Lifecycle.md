# 2. ML Lifecycle

```text
Business Problem
 ↓
Data Collection
 ↓
Preparation
 ↓
Feature Engineering
 ↓
Training
 ↓
Evaluation
 ↓
Deployment
 ↓
Monitoring
 ↓
Retraining
```

## Business problem

Example:
> Predict whether a vehicle will need service within seven days.

## Data

Identify sources, history, owners, refresh and quality requirements.

## Deployment

Predictions may be delivered by batch jobs, APIs or applications.

## Monitoring

Monitor both the pipeline and model behavior.

## Retraining

Retraining may be scheduled or triggered by defined conditions.

### Important
Production ML is a continuous lifecycle, not a one-time training script.
