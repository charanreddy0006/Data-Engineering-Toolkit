# 2. Batch Pipeline Lifecycle

```text
Discover
 ↓
Extract
 ↓
Stage
 ↓
Transform
 ↓
Validate
 ↓
Reconcile
 ↓
Publish
 ↓
Monitor
```

## Discover
Understand source ownership, schema, business meaning and SLA.

## Extract
Read data for the processing window.

## Stage
Preserve source data before applying business transformations.

## Transform
Apply standardization, joins, filters and calculations.

## Validate
Check schema, volume, values and relationships.

## Reconcile
Compare source and target counts or business totals.

## Publish
Expose trusted outputs to downstream users.

## Monitor
Track runtime, freshness, failures and quality.
