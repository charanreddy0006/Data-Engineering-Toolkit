# Architecture Review Template

## Problem
What business problem is being solved?

## Requirements
Volume, latency, retention, availability, security and cost.

## Proposed Design
Context, logical and physical diagrams.

## Alternatives
List realistic alternatives.

## Risks
Technical and operational risks.

## Security
Trust boundaries, identities and access.

## Operations
Monitoring, recovery, ownership and support.

## Cost
Major drivers and controls.

## Decision
Approved approach and assumptions.
