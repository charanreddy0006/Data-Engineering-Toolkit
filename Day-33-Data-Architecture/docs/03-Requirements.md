# 3. Architecture Requirements

Collect requirements before selecting technologies.

## Functional
What must the platform do?

## Non-functional
Measure:
- volume
- velocity
- variety
- latency
- retention
- availability
- durability
- security
- cost

## Questions
How many events per day? How quickly must they be available? How long are they retained? Who consumes them?

## Automobile
A connected-vehicle platform may have high event velocity but different latency needs for alerts, BI and historical analysis.
