# 22. Automobile Business Intelligence 🚗

## Business Domains

### Sales
- Vehicle sales
- Revenue
- Dealer performance
- Model mix

### Service
- Service orders
- Labor revenue
- Parts revenue
- Repair frequency
- Customer retention

### Warranty
- Claims
- Claim cost
- Claim rate
- Component failure patterns

### Connected Vehicles
- Trips
- Distance
- Utilization
- Fault events
- Energy consumption

### EV
- Charging sessions
- Energy delivered
- Charging duration
- Battery-related indicators

## Dashboard Suite

### Executive
Sales, revenue, service and warranty.

### Fleet
Utilization, distance, faults and vehicle health.

### Dealer
Service volume, repair order value and customer retention.

### EV
Charging activity and energy consumption.

## Key Design Rule
Keep raw telemetry, operational transactions and curated BI aggregates logically separated.
