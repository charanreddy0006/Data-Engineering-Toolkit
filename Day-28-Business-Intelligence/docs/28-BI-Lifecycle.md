# BI Lifecycle — Detailed Notes

## Step 1: Business Requirement
Translate vague requests into measurable questions.

Bad:
> Give me a sales dashboard.

Better:
> Show monthly vehicle sales, revenue and dealer contribution for the current fiscal year.

## Step 2: Source Discovery
Identify systems, owners, refresh frequency and data quality.

## Step 3: Modeling
Choose facts, dimensions and grain.

## Step 4: Metric Definition
Write formulas and edge cases.

## Step 5: Visualization
Choose visuals based on analytical questions.

## Step 6: Validation
Reconcile dashboard numbers against trusted source totals.

## Step 7: Security
Confirm each persona can see only approved data.

## Step 8: Deployment
Publish using controlled environments.

## Step 9: Monitoring
Track refresh, data quality and usage.

## Step 10: Continuous Improvement
Use feedback and telemetry to improve the dashboard.
