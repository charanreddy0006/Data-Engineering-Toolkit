# BI Architecture Checklist

## Purpose
Use this reference during Business Intelligence development and review.

## Checklist
- Define the business question.
- Confirm data source and ownership.
- Confirm fact grain.
- Confirm dimensions and relationships.
- Define metric formula and edge cases.
- Validate filters and time context.
- Check security.
- Check performance.
- Check freshness.
- Document assumptions.
- Record owner and support process.

## Automobile Reminder 🚗
Use realistic examples such as vehicle sales, dealer service, warranty claims, connected trips, faults and EV charging.
