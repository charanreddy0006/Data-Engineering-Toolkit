# Scalability

## 📖 Introduction

As businesses grow, the amount of data they generate also increases. Scalability ensures that systems continue to perform efficiently even when the workload grows.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you will:

- Understand scalability.
- Learn different scaling techniques.
- Know why scalability matters.

---

## 📌 What is Scalability?

Scalability is the ability of a system to handle increasing amounts of data, users, or requests without losing performance.

---

## 📈 Types of Scalability

### Vertical Scaling

Increase the power of a single server.

Examples:

- More CPU
- More RAM
- Larger Storage

---

### Horizontal Scaling

Add more servers to distribute the workload.

Most Big Data technologies use Horizontal Scaling.

---

## 🌍 Real-World Example

Netflix handles millions of users by adding more servers instead of upgrading a single machine.

---

## 🚗 Automobile Industry Example

As more connected vehicles come online, manufacturers add additional servers to process increased sensor data.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Scalability is essential for growing systems.
- Horizontal Scaling is preferred in Big Data.
- Cloud platforms make scaling easier.
