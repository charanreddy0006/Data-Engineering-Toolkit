# OLTP vs OLAP

## 📖 Introduction

Organizations use different database systems depending on their needs. **OLTP** systems handle daily business transactions, while **OLAP** systems support reporting and analytics.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you will:

- Understand OLTP and OLAP.
- Compare their features.
- Learn where they are used.

---

## 📌 What is OLTP?

**Online Transaction Processing (OLTP)** manages day-to-day transactions.

Examples:

- ATM Transactions
- Online Shopping
- Ticket Booking
- Banking Systems

Characteristics:

- Fast
- Small transactions
- High concurrency

---

## 📌 What is OLAP?

**Online Analytical Processing (OLAP)** is designed for reporting and business intelligence.

Examples:

- Sales Reports
- Revenue Analysis
- Customer Analytics
- Business Dashboards

Characteristics:

- Complex queries
- Large datasets
- Historical analysis

---

## 📊 Comparison

| Feature | OLTP | OLAP |
|---------|------|------|
| Purpose | Transactions | Analytics |
| Data | Current | Historical |
| Queries | Simple | Complex |
| Speed | Very Fast | Optimized for Analysis |

---

## 🌍 Real-World Example

An e-commerce website:

- Customer purchases → OLTP
- Monthly sales dashboard → OLAP

---

## 🚗 Automobile Industry Example

A dealership records every vehicle sale in an OLTP database.

Management analyzes yearly sales trends using an OLAP system.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- OLTP supports daily operations.
- OLAP supports business analysis.
- Both systems work together in modern organizations.
