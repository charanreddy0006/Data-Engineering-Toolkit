# Beginner Interview Questions

## 1. What is data quality?
Fitness of data for intended use.

## 2. What is completeness?
Presence of required data.

## 3. What is uniqueness?
Whether supposedly unique identifiers are duplicated.

## 4. Is validity the same as accuracy?
No. A value can be within the allowed range but still be wrong.

## 5. What is referential integrity?
Relationships point to valid referenced entities.

## 6. Why quarantine bad data?
To preserve evidence for audit, debugging, replay, and remediation.

## 7. What is freshness?
How recently data was updated.

## 8. What is a data contract?
An agreement covering schema, semantics, quality, ownership, and delivery expectations.

## 9. Why are duplicates dangerous?
They can inflate counts, sums, joins, and downstream metrics.
