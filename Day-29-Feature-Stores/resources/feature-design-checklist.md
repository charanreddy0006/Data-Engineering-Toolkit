# Feature Design Checklist

Before publishing a feature, answer:

1. What business/model problem does it solve?
2. What is the entity?
3. What is the timestamp?
4. What is the transformation?
5. What is the window?
6. What data is excluded?
7. Can leakage occur?
8. What is the freshness requirement?
9. What quality rules apply?
10. Who owns it?
11. Which models consume it?
12. Is online serving required?
13. What does a backfill do?
14. What is the cost?
