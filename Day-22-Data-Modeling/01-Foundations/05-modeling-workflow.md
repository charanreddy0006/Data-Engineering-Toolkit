# End-to-End Modeling Workflow

A disciplined workflow reduces rework.

## 1. Gather requirements

Identify:

- users;
- business questions;
- source systems;
- expected latency;
- historical requirements;
- retention.

## 2. Identify business processes

Examples:

```text
Trip
Service
Charging
Warranty
Manufacturing
```

## 3. Define grain

Write the grain sentence before columns.

## 4. Identify dimensions

Ask who, what, where, and when.

## 5. Identify measures

Ask what can be counted, summed, averaged, or derived.

## 6. Define history

Decide which attributes require historical versions.

## 7. Define quality

Specify uniqueness, validity, completeness, referential, and freshness rules.

## 8. Choose physical design

Only after understanding workload and scale.

## 9. Validate

Use sample data and trusted business totals.

## 10. Document

Publish the model, dictionary, lineage, quality, and change policy.
