# Data Modeling Interview — Beginner

## What is data modeling?

Designing data structure, relationships, constraints, and semantics for a defined workload.

## What is a primary key?

A unique row identifier.

## What is a foreign key?

A reference to a key in another table.

## What is normalization?

Reducing unnecessary redundancy and anomalies.

## What is a fact?

An analytical representation of a business process at a defined grain.

## What is a dimension?

Descriptive context used to slice facts.

## What is grain?

The exact meaning of one row.

## What is SCD Type 2?

A history-preserving dimension technique that creates a new version when tracked attributes change.

## Why is grain important?

Because joins and aggregations must preserve business meaning.
