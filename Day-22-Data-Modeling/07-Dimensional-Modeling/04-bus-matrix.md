## Why it matters

It reveals:

- reusable dimensions;
- duplicated dimensions;
- missing dimensions;
- cross-domain reporting opportunities.

## Workshop method

For each process ask:

1. What is the event?
2. What is the grain?
3. What measures exist?
4. Which dimensions describe it?
5. Which dimensions should be conformed?
