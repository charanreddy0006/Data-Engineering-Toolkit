# Scenario Security Interviews

## Purpose
Practice realistic situations.

## Credential leak

A database credential is committed to Git. Explain immediate containment, revocation/rotation, exposure assessment, log review, cleanup, and prevention.

## Excessive access

An analyst downloads millions of customer-linked rows. Explain detection, authorization review, containment, evidence, investigation, and escalation.

## Kafka permissions

A telemetry topic is readable by too many teams. Explain inventory, consumer review, ACL redesign, migration, and monitoring.
